Good to know

Questions.

Quick answers about compatibility, privacy and getting help.

Is this allowed on servers?

Konga Style is visual only. It changes what you see on your own screen and does not provide gameplay advantages. Server rules vary, so check the rules of the server you play on.

How do I update?

When an update is available, the menu shows a download button. Choose your Minecraft version above and install the new jar in your mods folder.

Why does a scanner warn about Konga Style?

Some scanners flag Java mods or native loading in general. The full version's Spotify card reads the song title from the Spotify window. The Clean version leaves that feature out. The project is not obfuscated and its full source is on GitHub.

What does Konga Style send over the internet?

Styles and sky textures are fetched from the cloud when you choose them. Your cosmetics are local to your screen. The Spotify card reads the currently playing song from the Spotify window when you use the Full version.

Does it work on Lunar or Badlion?

The mod requires Fabric Loader and Fabric API. Launcher compatibility can depend on the Minecraft version and the launcher's mod support.

Can other players see my cosmetics?

No. Capes, auras and other cosmetics are client-side and only visible to you.

Can I share or change Konga Style?

The source code is available on GitHub. Check the project's license for the rules on sharing and changes.

How do I get help?

Join the Discord community for help with installation, styles and suggestions.
